About Retriev :

Retriev is a local-first AI knowledge assistant designed to help you organize, index, search, and interact with your personal documents using artificial intelligence.

Import your files and folders into dedicated workspaces. Retriev processes your documents, creates searchable representations, and lets you ask questions about your information through AI-powered conversations.

Built as a Windows desktop application, Retriev combines local storage, semantic search, vector-based retrieval, and AI integrations to make your personal knowledge accessible from one place.

What can Retriev do?

Everything you need to build and interact with your own searchable knowledge base.

Local-First Architecture

Keep your workspaces and indexed knowledge on your own device, with a local backend and storage system.

Workspace Organization

Create separate workspaces to organize documents and keep different collections of knowledge apart.

File & Folder Import

Import individual files or entire folders from accessible locations on your Windows computer, including nested folders.

Document Indexing

Process supported documents, divide their content into searchable chunks, and prepare them for AI-powered retrieval.

Semantic Search

Search your knowledge by meaning and context, rather than relying only on exact keyword matches.

AI-Powered Conversations

Ask questions about your indexed documents and retrieve relevant information through contextual AI conversations.

Local Embeddings

Use a locally packaged MiniLM embedding model to convert document content into vector representations for semantic retrieval.

Gemini & Ollama Integration

Connect to Google's Gemini models or use compatible locally hosted models through Ollama.

Vector-Based Retrieval

Use LanceDB-powered vector storage to organize and retrieve relevant document chunks.

Managed Document Copies

Imported documents are copied into their workspace-managed storage. Original source files remain untouched.

Retriev is currently in beta. Updates, improvements, and additional features are ongoing.

Your documents and workspaces are managed locally on your device. When using cloud-based AI services such as Gemini, relevant document content may be sent to the selected provider to generate responses.
